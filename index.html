Privacy Policy for Hear La La

Effective date: 2026-05-06

This Privacy Policy explains how Hear La La ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Hear La La app.

Hear La La does not require users to create an account or log in. The main communication features are designed to work like an offline application, and user-created content such as saved phrases is stored locally on the user's device.

Information We Collect or Process

Hear La La may collect, process, or store the following types of information, depending on how users use the app:

  • Information you enter into the app to support communication.
  • Saved phrases, text, or other communication-related content you choose to save for later use.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support, feedback, or other requests.
  • Limited technical, diagnostic, or usage information that may be processed by app stores or platform services, such as Google Play, Google Play Services, the Apple App Store, or Apple platform services, according to their own privacy policies.

We do not require users to provide personal information, create an account, or log in to use the main features of the app.

Saved phrases and communication-related content are stored locally on the user's device and are not stored on our own server.

How We Use Information

We use or process information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Hear La La communication tools.
  • Let users save and reuse phrases or information that fit their lifestyle.
  • Make communication easier for hearing-impaired users and the people who communicate with them.
  • Respond to user questions, feedback, or support requests.
  • Comply with legal, security, or policy requirements.

Local Storage

Hear La La allows users to save phrases or communication-related information for later use. This information is stored locally on the user's device using the app's local storage.

These saved phrases are not stored on our own server and are not linked to a user account, because Hear La La does not require users to create an account or log in.

Users can manage or delete saved phrases inside the app. Users can also remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage from the device settings or uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services and App Stores

Hear La La does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or crash reporting services in the app.

The app may be distributed through app stores such as Google Play and the Apple App Store. These app stores and related platform services may process limited technical, device, purchase, download, diagnostic, or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

You can review their privacy policies here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data.

We do not share locally stored saved phrases or user-created communication data with our own server, because Hear La La does not operate its own server for storing this data.

Limited technical, diagnostic, or usage information may be processed by third-party services that help provide, maintain, secure, or improve the app, such as app distribution, diagnostics, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or technical infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or others.

Data Security

Hear La La does not require users to create an account or log in, and the app does not operate its own server for storing user-created communication data.

User-created content, such as saved phrases or communication-related information, is stored locally on the user's device. The security of this local data depends partly on the security of the user's device and operating system.

We take reasonable steps to design the app in a way that protects locally stored information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Users should keep their devices secure by using a screen lock, keeping the operating system updated, and avoiding rooted, jailbroken, or compromised devices, because those conditions may weaken the protection of the app and locally stored data.

Data Retention

Saved phrases and communication-related information are stored locally on the user's device using the app's local storage. This data is retained on the device until the user deletes it inside the app, clears the app's storage from the device settings, or uninstalls the app.

Hear La La does not require users to create an account or log in, and we do not operate our own server to permanently store saved phrases or user-created communication data.

If users voluntarily contact us by email for support, feedback, or other requests, we may retain those email messages and related information for up to 12 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer retention period is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or compliance reasons.

Basic technical, crash, diagnostic, analytics, or usage data collected through third-party services may be retained according to those third-party providers' own retention policies.

Data Deletion

Users can delete saved phrases or communication-related information inside the app. Users can also delete locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage from the device settings or uninstalling the app.

Because Hear La La does not require an account, there is no account deletion process inside the app.

If users voluntarily contact us by email and provide personal information, they may request deletion of that information by contacting us at quickslothenglishstudio@gmail.com .

Please include the app name "Hear La La" in your request so we can identify the relevant information.

After receiving a deletion request for information provided directly to us, we will delete or anonymize the relevant personal data within a reasonable period, unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or compliance reasons.

Children's Privacy

Hear La La may be used by younger users with the supervision of a parent, guardian, teacher, or caregiver.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete the information if required.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

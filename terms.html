About Hear La La

Hear La La is designed to support people with hearing impairments and people who need to communicate with them. The app provides tools that help users create two-way communication, save useful phrases or communication-related information, and reuse them later.

The app does not require users to create an account or log in. The main features are designed to work like an offline application, and saved phrases or user-created communication content are stored locally on the user's device.
